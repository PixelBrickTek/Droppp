Droppp help

Use Droppp in three steps.

Keep your iPhone and sending device on the same Wi-Fi network, then send files through the local drop page.

Download on App Store Start with step 1 Back to home

1, 2, 3

From browser to iPhone.

Droppp works locally, so the setup stays short and predictable.

1

Open Droppp on iPhone

Launch Droppp and turn on receiving. The app shows a local web address and a QR code for nearby devices.

2

Connect from the browser

On your computer or another device, enter the address shown in Droppp or scan the QR code while using the same Wi-Fi network.

3

Drop files and manage them

Choose or drag files into the browser page. Once received, files appear in Droppp where you can share or delete them.

Before sending

Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network. If the page does not open, check that receiving is still enabled in Droppp.

After receiving

Files remain inside Droppp until you share, delete, or remove the app. Nothing is uploaded to an external cloud service.
