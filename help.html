DROPPP / THE GUIDE

Your first drop.
And every one after.

Connect over Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot. Open a browser, move your files, and keep them where you need them.

Download on App Store ↗Start the guide ↓

Keep Droppp open and your screen on. Both devices must stay connected through Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot.

Choose your connection.

Same Wi-Fi

Connect your iPhone or iPad and the other device to the same Wi-Fi network.

Personal Hotspot

On your iPhone or supported cellular iPad, open Settings → Personal Hotspot and turn on Allow Others to Join. Connect the other device to that hotspot.

Once connected, open Droppp. Keep the app visible and the screen on throughout the transfer.

01

Switch on the local server.

Turn on the switch beside Droppp. Wait for the connection address to appear. Droppp remembers your ON/OFF preference when you reopen the app; a supported network connection is still needed.

02

Open your address in a browser.

On the device connected to your Wi-Fi or hotspot, enter the full address shown in Droppp. A computer, phone, or tablet can connect without installing another app.

Copy, scan, or change the port.

Tap the address to expand the controls beneath it. Copy the link, show a QR code, or change the server port. Tap the address again to collapse the controls.

Your address may use numbers or a name ending in .local. Always use the current address and port shown on your own device, not the one in a screenshot.

03

Upload here. Download there.

Browse your Droppp library, Files, or a connected folder from the web page.

Send files to Droppp.

Open the destination folder, then drop files onto the page or choose them with the upload button. On a phone or tablet, use the file picker.

Download to the other device.

Download individual files, select several, or download a folder. The browser saves the download on that device.

Create text and links.

Use the browser’s TXT and URL controls to save a text file or a web link in the selected location.

If a transfer stops after you leave the app or lock the screen, return to Droppp, check that the server is ON, and reconnect using the displayed address.

04

Keep your files in order.

Use + to import photos or files, create a folder, or write a TXT file. Create folders inside other folders and drag items into place. Search the current folder and filter by type, extension, or size.

You can also drag files into Droppp from other apps. Select saved files to share, delete, or move them into apps that support drag and drop.

05

Open the same files in Files.

Tap Files below the + button in Droppp. This dedicated folder is also available in Apple’s Files app.

Find it on your device.

In Files, open Browse → On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Droppp → Files. Additions and deletions inside this folder appear in both apps.

Copy between your libraries.

Drag files from Droppp’s main list onto Files to copy them. To copy back, hold one or more files, close the Files page with X, and drop them into the main list.

The Files shortcut cannot be deleted inside Droppp. Its contents can be deleted. If the folder is removed in the Files app, Droppp recreates the folder when reopened; deleted files are not restored.

06

Connect an existing folder.

Tap + → Connect a folder, then choose a folder in the Files picker. It appears in your list with a small link indicator.

Work with the original files.

A connected folder points to its original location. Files added or deleted inside it are added or deleted there, too. Disconnecting the folder removes its link from Droppp and keeps the original folder.

Use it from your browser.

Open the connected folder on Droppp’s web page to upload, browse, and download its files.

Include iCloud Drive.

You can connect iCloud Drive folders. Files stored only in the cloud may need time and an internet connection to download before they open or transfer.

07

Choose what to record.

Open History from Settings to review recent file activity and transfer insights.

Find an activity.

Review received, sent, and deleted items. Search by file name or source, then filter by activity, time, or file type.

Pause new records.

Turn on Disable history below History in Settings. New activity is no longer recorded, and existing history is kept. Turn it off to resume recording.

Remove old records.

Delete selected history entries when you no longer need them. Removing a record does not delete the file.

08

Make it feel like yours.

Open Settings with the gear button.

Appearance and language.

Follow your system appearance or choose Light or Dark. Select your preferred app language here, too.

Auto-dim screen.

Lower screen brightness automatically while the server is on. Keep Droppp in the foreground for transfers.

Fewer confirmation messages.

Turn on Hide import success message below Auto-dim screen to skip the import completion popup. It is off by default. Choosing Don’t show again in that popup enables the same setting.

09

A few useful details.

Quick answers for your next transfer.

Why can’t the browser connect?

Check that both devices share the same Wi-Fi or that the other device is connected to your Personal Hotspot. Keep Droppp open with the server ON and allow its Local Network permission in iOS Settings. Enter the full current address, including http:// and the port. Some guest or public Wi-Fi networks prevent devices from connecting to each other.

Does a hotspot link work from anywhere?

No. The other device must be connected to your hotspot. The address shown in Droppp is a local address, not a public website.

Do hotspot transfers use mobile data?

Transferring files already stored on your device stays on the local connection. Downloading cloud-only files, syncing a connected cloud folder, or other internet activity may use mobile data.

How do I select all files?

After selecting files, tap the selected item count in the lower-left corner to select all files.

Can I change the server port?

Tap the address to expand its controls, choose the port option, and enter a new port. Reconnect using the updated address.

Where are my files stored?

Your Droppp library and its Files folder are stored on your device and are removed if you delete the app. Connected folders stay in their original location. A connected cloud folder can sync through its storage provider.

Do I need an account or a cloud service?

Droppp needs no account or cloud service for local transfers. Connecting an iCloud Drive folder is optional and uses your existing iCloud setup.

What if a connected folder is unavailable?

Check that it opens in the Files app and that its storage provider is available. For iCloud files, check your internet connection. If access has changed, reconnect the folder from the + menu.
