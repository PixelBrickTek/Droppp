01 / THE CONNECTION

Wi-Fi or hotspot.
One simple flow.

Send photos, videos, documents, and ZIPs from a computer, phone, or tablet. All the other device needs is a browser.

AT HOME OR AT WORK

Use the same Wi-Fi.

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network, then open Droppp.

WITHOUT A WI-FI ROUTER

Use Personal Hotspot.

Enable Personal Hotspot on your iPhone or supported iPad. Connect the other device to that hotspot, then open Droppp.

  1. 01

    Switch on.

    Open Droppp and turn on the local server.

  2. 02

    Open the address.

    Use the exact address shown in Droppp on your connected device, or scan the QR code.

  3. 03

    Send or download.

    Drop files into the browser to upload, or browse your saved files and download them.

Keep Droppp open and your iPhone or iPad screen on while connected. Read the connection guide →
