Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

Droppp is designed as a simple local Wi‑Fi file transfer app. We respect your privacy and do not collect, store, or share personal information.

Information We Collect

Droppp does not collect personal information. The app does not require account registration, login, email address, name, or any other personal identifier.

File Transfers

Droppp allows files to be transferred between devices on the same local Wi‑Fi network. Files transferred through Droppp are stored locally on your device inside the app’s data storage.

Droppp does not upload your files to any external server, cloud service, or third-party storage.

Local Network

Droppp may use your local network to allow nearby devices on the same Wi‑Fi network to access the temporary file drop page provided by the app. This connection is used only for local file transfer while the receiving feature is active.

Third-Party Services

Droppp does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or external data collection services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information because Droppp does not collect personal information.

Data Storage and Deletion

Files received through Droppp remain stored locally on your device unless you delete them from the app or remove the app from your device.

Children’s Privacy

Droppp does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at brick@pixelbricktek.com.
